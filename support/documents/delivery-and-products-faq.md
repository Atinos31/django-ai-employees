# VoltHub delivery and product FAQ

## Delivery

We deliver across Europe with DHL Express, DPD and bpost. Delivery usually takes 2 to 5 working days after dispatch. As soon as your order is dispatched, the carrier and tracking number appear on your order page, and Aura can check the live tracking status for you.

If tracking shows your parcel as delivered but you cannot find it, check with neighbours and any safe place the carrier may have used, then tell Aura in the support chat.

## Payment

Payment is handled securely by Stripe. The payment methods available to you are shown at checkout. Prices are shown in euros.

## TVs

The VoltHub Vision 43" 4K Smart TV has HDR10, built-in streaming apps, 3 HDMI ports and a voice remote. The Vision 55" QLED TV adds Dolby Vision, 120Hz motion and Dolby Atmos sound. The Vision 65" 4K Smart TV has HDR10+, 60W speakers, a low latency gaming mode and comes with a wall mount kit.

## Phones and tablets

The VoltHub Nova 5G Phone 128GB has a 6.5 inch AMOLED screen, a 50MP triple camera, a 5000mAh battery and 33W fast charging. The Nova Pro 5G Phone 256GB has a 6.7 inch 120Hz AMOLED screen, a 200MP camera, wireless charging and IP68 water resistance. The VoltHub Tab 11 Tablet has an 11 inch 2K screen, 8GB RAM, 256GB storage, stylus support and quad speakers.

## Laptops

The VoltHub AirBook 14 has an Intel Core i5, 16GB RAM, a 512GB SSD and a backlit keyboard. The ProBook 16 has an Intel Core i7, 32GB RAM, a 1TB SSD and dedicated graphics.

## Accessories

The VoltHub Buds Pro Wireless Earbuds have active noise cancellation and up to 30 hours of battery with the case. The VoltHub 25W USB-C Fast Charger includes a 1m USB-C cable and fast-charges phones and tablets.
