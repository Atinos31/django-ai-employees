# VoltHub warranty policy

## 2-year legal guarantee

Every product sold by VoltHub is covered by the EU legal guarantee for 2 years from delivery. If a product develops a fault that was not caused by misuse, an accident or liquid damage, we repair it, replace it or refund it.

## How to make a warranty claim

Open the order on your VoltHub orders page and chat with Aura, our support assistant. Describe the fault and when it started. Aura checks your order and passes the claim to our support manager, who decides on a repair, a replacement or a refund.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage after delivery, such as drops, cracked screens or liquid damage beyond the product's water-resistance rating. Normal wear such as scratches on the casing. Damage caused by unofficial repairs or modifications. Software problems that a reset or update fixes.

## Batteries

Batteries in phones, tablets, laptops and earbuds are covered for faults for the full 2 years. A gradual loss of battery capacity with normal use is expected and is not a fault.
