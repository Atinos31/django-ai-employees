# VoltHub refund and returns policy

VoltHub sells TVs, phones, laptops, tablets and accessories to customers across Europe. All prices are in euros (EUR). This policy follows EU consumer rules.

## Changing your mind (14-day right of withdrawal)

You can cancel an online order for any reason within 14 days of receiving it. Tell us through the support chat on your order page. Return the product complete, with its accessories and packaging, within 14 days of telling us. When you change your mind, you pay the return shipping.

## Damaged on arrival

If a product arrives damaged, report it in the support chat within 14 days of delivery, ideally with photos of the product and the packaging. We pay the return shipping and refund the full price, or send a replacement if you prefer.

## Orders that have not arrived

If your order has not been delivered more than 14 days after you placed it, you can ask for a full refund. We check the carrier's tracking first. If the carrier reports a delivery you did not receive, our team looks into it with the carrier before deciding.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of approval. Some requests, for example on high-value orders, are reviewed by a member of our team before a decision is made. We let you know when the review is finished.

## Cancelled orders

If an order was cancelled before dispatch, its payment was already returned at cancellation, so there is nothing further to refund.
